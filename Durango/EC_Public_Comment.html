You have explored Durango's existing conditions. Now help guide what comes next. Drop a pin on the map to provide your input on each theme to help shape the plan.

Please leave a written comment with every pin. A few words about your pin adds weight and gives the planning team the story behind it.

Click anywhere outside this window to close the window and leave a comment.

Drop a pin in six steps

  1. Close this window by clicking outside the box.
  2. Zoom and pan the map to the area you care about or where you want to leave a comment.
  3. Pick your question. In the Editor widget, select the flag for the question you want to answer:
    Where could Durango add homes for the people who live and work here?
    Where in Durango do you feel most connected to nature or where should we focus on protecting nature?
    Where in Durango do you feel the local culture and economy most?
    Where in town is it hardest to get around and get the services you need?
  4. Click once to drop your pin. A crosshair will appear on your screen.
  5. Type a comment. A comment box will appear and a comment is required to drop a pin. It can be short, and it helps support your pin.
  6. Click Create to save your pin.
Comment as much as you likeTo answer a different question, click Create, then use the back arrow in the top left corner of the Editor widget to see all the options again.
Pins are permanentYou cannot delete a pin once it is placed, and you cannot edit other people's comments.
