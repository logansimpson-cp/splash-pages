How should the region move through 2055?

A joint planning effort of the Bullhead City Metropolitan Planning Organization and the City of Bullhead City

Two plans are being developed together. Bullhead in Motion 2055, the Regional Transportation Plan, is led by the BHCMPO and looks at the whole regional network — roads, intersections, bicycle and pedestrian facilities, and freight. Bullhead Bound, the Short-Range Transit Plan, is led by the City of Bullhead City and focuses on transit service, stops, and routes over the next five years.

Because the two plans share a study area and a public process, one comment map serves both. Pin a transit comment and it goes to Bullhead Bound; pin anything else and it goes to Bullhead in Motion 2055.

Your comments help shape the projects this plan carries forward.

Drop a pin in four steps

Pick a pin from the panel

The editor panel is already open on the right. Click the category that matches what you want to tell us about.

Click the spot on the map

Pan and zoom to the street, intersection, or stop, then click to drop your pin there.

Type your comment

A comment box opens with the pin. Tell us what you would change and why.

Click Create to save

Your pin and comment are not saved until you click Create. Once saved, they appear on the map for everyone to see.

Place as many pins as you like. The comment is the most useful part: "Hard to cross here at school pickup" tells the planning team far more than a pin on its own.
