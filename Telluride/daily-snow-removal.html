Snow Removal Schedule

The daily snow removal schedule is posted here around 5:00 pm MT each day. When in doubt, please adhere to posted street signs when considering whether to move your vehicle for snow removal.

What each status means
Tow and Haul Zone

Cars will be ticketed and towed.

Ticketing Zone

Cars will be ticketed; the crew will work around them.

No Snow Removal Today

Parking is allowed.

On any day without active removal: streets stay open for parking unless marked otherwise for Skijoring detour routes or street sweeping.
