It takes about a minute

Adding your pin

Use the Add a Comment panel to place a pin. Nothing saves until the last step, so look around first.

👇

Look for this box on the left side of your screen

On the map page, scroll down the panel on the left side past the green welcome message. You'll come to a box called Editor. That's the box outlined in red below.

Right under it are the words Add a Comment! and a list of six topics. Tap or click any topic in that list to start placing a pin.

1

Pick a question

In the Add a Comment panel, choose the one of six that matches what you want to say. That switches on pin placement and the comment box.

2

Click the spot you mean

Place your pin on the exact block, building, trail, or corner you have in mind.

3

Tell us why

Add a sentence or two. This is the part that helps most: the pin shows where, your comment shows why it matters.

4

Save it, then add more

Submit and drop as many pins as you like across any of the six questions. Everything is saved without a name or login attached.

Come back anytime

The map stays open through this phase of the Comprehensive Plan. If something occurs to you next week, add it then.

What each pin asks

The six questions

Each one is tied to a part of the Plan. Answer the ones you have something to say about and skip the rest.

Community

Where do you feel most at home or most connected to the community in Telluride?

Getting Around

Where are there opportunities to improve pedestrian, biking, or public transit networks?

Livability

Where in Town could there be enhancements to increase livability for year-round residents?

Health & Wellbeing

Where would better access to health care, wellness services, or fresh and affordable food help you most?

Economy

Where would you like to see more local, year-round business, and what type?

Environment & Recreation

Which open space, trail, natural area, or recreational resource would you most want protected, enhanced, or expanded as Telluride grows?

Your pins go straight to the planning team and help set the priorities in the Comprehensive Plan. Thanks for sharing your Telluride.
